# THE LAST SEED (የመጨረሻው ዘር)
**Duration:** 90 Seconds | **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 | **Language:** Amharic (English Subtitles)  
**Genre:** Ethiopian Historical Folktale / Magical Realism  
**Visual Style:** Grounded Ethiopian highland realism, natural earth-to-emerald color progression, slow deliberate camera work.

---

## 1. Characters & Visual Anchors

* **Alemu (አለሙ) — The Old Farmer (Age 70 → 85 in final scene)**
  * **Look:** Deeply weathered dark skin, etched lines around calm, observant eyes, close-cropped grey hair and beard. Wears a worn hand-spun white cotton *shamma* (ሻማ) with a dark earth-toned *gabi* (ጋቢ) draped over one shoulder and worn footwear.
  * **Arc:** Begins frail with exhaustion, finds quiet resolve in the seed, and ages gracefully into an elder sitting beneath his legacy.
* **The Villagers (3–5 Adults)**
  * **Look:** Sun-worn faces, braided hair or simple wraps, dusty cotton *shammas* and tunics, holding a dry clay water vessel and idle tools. Not antagonists—exhausted neighbors acting out of survival instinct.
* **The Young Girl (Age 8–9, Final Scene)**
  * **Look:** Traditional braided hairstyle (*ሹሩባ*), light cotton dress with subtle border trim. Represents the generation born into the shade and harvest Alemu planted.
* **The Seed & The Tree (*ዋርካ* / *ዋንዛ*)**
  * **Continuity:** A single dark-brown, textured indigenous seed. No artificial glow. When grown, it becomes a sprawling highland tree heavy with leaves and fruit, surrounded by lush green crops and woven harvest baskets (*ሰፌድ*/*ቅርጫት*) shared by the community.

## 4. Production & AI-Generation Guardrails

* **Color Grading Arc:** Scene 01–04 (desaturated ochre, burnt sienna, harsh high-key sun) → Scene 05–06 (cool slate grey, deep wet umber, soft morning gold) → Scene 07–08 (lush emerald greens, warm amber sunbeams).
* **Instrumentation:** Keep the score strictly grounded in the historical setting—solo *washint* (flute), plucked *krar* (lyre), low *masinko* (single-string fiddle) drones, and subtle *kebero* heartbeat percussion. Avoid modern synthesizers or Western piano.
* **Strict Negative Constraints:** No modern clothing, plastic containers, metal roofs, readable text in generated frames, glowing/fantasy magic effects on the seed, or instant time-lapse tree growth.
